Insider Travel Tips & Mini-Guides to Corsica

🌅 Calvi

Calvi is a beautiful seaside town known for its medieval citadel and sweeping bay views. Walk along the harbor, sip espresso by the sea, and enjoy live music at night.

Tip: Climb to the top of the citadel at sunset for unforgettable views.

🏞️ Bonifacio

Perched atop white limestone cliffs, Bonifacio feels like a postcard come to life. Wander the old town, explore sea caves, or take a boat tour to the nearby Lavezzi islands.

Tip: Don’t miss the King Aragon’s Steps carved into the cliffs - 187 steps of scenic effort!

🏖️ Porto-Vecchio

This upscale beach town blends chic resorts with pristine nature. Palombaggia and Santa Giulia beaches are among the best in Europe.

Tip: Visit off-season for a quieter, more affordable stay.

⛰️ Corte

Located in the mountainous heart of Corsica, Corte is ideal for hikers and nature lovers. It’s also home to the University of Corsica, giving it a youthful vibe.

Tip: Explore the Restonica Valley - a rugged, turquoise river canyon with great swimming holes.

🍷 General Travel Tips

  • Rent a small car – Corsican roads are often narrow and twisty.
  • Learn a few French or Corsican phrases – it helps tremendously in rural areas.
  • Respect the local pace of life – shops may close midday and reopen later.
  • Always carry water, especially if hiking in summer.
